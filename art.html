Knock Knock 世界 (Podcast)

I joined Knock Knock World, a teen-focused podcast produced by ShengFM（声动活泼） as a freelance writer — my first attempt at putting out content on a long-term basis. Since then I've helped make dozens of episodes, getting to know firsthand both the thrill and the difficulty of the creative process, while continuing to explore what audio offers as a medium and what topics resonate with teenage listeners. I've been lucky enough to interview interesting people like a science-fiction writer and a rocket engineer — and even luckier to find, in the kids' endless curious questions, a steady source of inspiration and some hope for the future of this turbulent world.

My writing centers on environmental, social, and scientific topics. Drawing on news, research papers, books, and documentaries, I try to translate current events for 9-to-13-year-olds, helping them broaden how they see the world and build the habit of thinking for themselves.
